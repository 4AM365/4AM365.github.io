---
title: Focaccia Calculator
---

A baker's-percentage build sheet for high-hydration focaccia — punch in your
flour weight and it scales water, salt, yeast, semolina, lamination oil, and
the salamoia brine, with long-ferment and same-day options.

A **prep timeline** lays each topping onto the dough's own clock — showing the
long ferment as your window to roast tomatoes, toast crumbs and infuse garlic,
and calling out what has to cool or dry before it goes on. The same prep is also
listed linearly, in order, as a tick-as-you-go checklist.
