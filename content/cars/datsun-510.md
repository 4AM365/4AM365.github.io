title: Datsun 510 — hillclimb build
---

Mike's hillclimb 510. Full chassis electrical system designed and wired from scratch.

## Scope

- Full chassis harness — engine, lighting, ignition, charging, fuel pump, gauges, motorsport switches.
- Relay/fuse panel, master cutoff, emergency-pull-handle compliant.
- Labeled connectors and replaceable sections for trackside diagnosis.
- Sensor integration: gauges and data-logging touchpoints with analog signal conditioning where needed.
